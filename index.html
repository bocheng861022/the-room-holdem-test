STANDARD RULES

Texas Hold’em

Each player receives two private cards. Five community cards are dealt in stages: flop, turn, and river. Make the best five-card hand using any combination of your private and community cards.

Action moves clockwise through four betting rounds. You may check, call, raise, or fold when legal. The best remaining hand wins at showdown; if all others fold, the last player wins without showing.
