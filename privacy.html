THE ROOM

Privacy Policy

Effective September 3, 2026

The current release works offline, requires no account, contains no advertising or analytics SDK, and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the publisher or third parties.

Data on your device

Preferences and play-money balances may be stored locally on your device and can be removed by deleting the app or clearing its local data. Your operating-system provider may include app data in a device backup according to your device and backup settings; the publisher cannot access that backup.

Permissions

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, tracking, or notification access.

Children

The Room is intended for adults and depicts simulated gambling using valueless virtual chips. It is not directed to children.

Changes
