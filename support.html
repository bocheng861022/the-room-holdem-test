THE ROOM

Player Support

Before contacting support, restart the app. If a hand appears stuck, return to the lobby and take a new seat. Local progress can be reset by clearing the app's local data.

Game rules

The Room follows standard No-Limit Texas Hold’em with a 52-card deck. Tap “How to Play” in the lobby for the hand order and betting overview.

Contact

A monitored publisher support email and public support URL must be inserted before store submission.

Safety

No purchases, deposits, withdrawals, cash prizes, transfers, or real-money gambling are available. If play stops feeling recreational, take a break or remove the app.
